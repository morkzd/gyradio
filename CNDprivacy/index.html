Privacy Policy

Cricket Nets Duel · Last updated: November, 2026

Cricket Nets Duel by J Simeti Games ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app and the website at gyradio.com/netsduel.

In short: no accounts, no tracking, no ads, nothing stored on our side. The only thing that ever leaves your device is a first name, and only so the app can speak it aloud.

What stays on your device

Player names and scores are held in memory while you play. They are not stored and are not sent to us. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

What leaves your device

To generate the spoken commentary, the app sends a short line of text to Google's text-to-speech service. That request includes the text itself — which may contain a name you typed — and your device's IP address. Google handles it under its own privacy policy.

You can turn all sound off at any time using the Sound button, which stops those requests.

What we don't do

  • No analytics
  • No tracking
  • No advertising
  • No cookies in the app

Website

The website is a static page. It does not set cookies. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses, for security and reliability.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and has no accounts. The only personal detail ever transmitted is a first name typed on the setup screen, and only to generate audio. Nothing is stored, so there is no profile of a child to build up over time.

Your rights

Because we hold no data ourselves, there is no stored record to request, correct or erase. Requests made to Google under its policy are handled by Google.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes, this policy will be updated on this page and the date above revised.
