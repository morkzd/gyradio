Privacy Policy

GYRADIO · Last updated: November 2026

Glory Years Radio by J Simeti Games ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the website at gyradio.com.

In short: no accounts, no tracking, no ads. Your favourites stay on your own device. The only third party involved is Suno, which supplies the songs.

What stays on your device

Favourites are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to us, and there is no sync or backup. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Clearing your browser data removes your favourites permanently.

What leaves your device

Songs play through Suno's embedded player, which loads from suno.com. Suno sees your IP address and may set its own cookies or storage on your device. Suno handles that under its own privacy policy.

Nothing about which songs you pick is sent to us — the choice is made in your browser.

What we don't do

  • No analytics
  • No tracking
  • No advertising
  • No accounts or profiles

The song list

The catalogue is a plain file served from our own site. Fetching it reveals your IP to our host in the ordinary way, and nothing more.

Server logs

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses, for security and reliability.

Children's privacy

The site is suitable for all ages and has no accounts. Nothing is collected beyond what is described above, and the only thing stored locally is the list of favourites the user chose.

Your rights

Because we hold no data ourselves, there is no stored record to request, correct or erase. Anything Suno holds is handled by Suno under its policy.

Changes to this policy

If the site's behaviour changes, this policy will be updated on this page and the date above revised.
