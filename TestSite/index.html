How to Play

Put an object behind the bowler — a bag, a pad, a box. That's the bonus target.

Each player gets 18 balls and starts on 100 points.

  • Top nets (+30) — ball hits the ceiling net. The bowler can catch you off the rebound, and that counts as an out.
  • Back nets (-10) — ball gets past you and hits the nets behind.
  • Bonus target — you hit the object: back to 100 points.
  • Out (-25) — a real dismissal, including caught off the nets.
  • Wide (+10) — no ball used up.
  • Nothing — anywhere else. No change.

If you're over 100 points, the bonus target becomes something to avoid — hitting it drags you back down to 100.

Score can't go below 0. Highest score at the end wins.
